Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2026

At Hadi88, we are committed to protecting user privacy and maintaining transparency. This Privacy Policy explains how data is collected, processed, stored, and protected when you use our platform.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Name, username, email address, encrypted password, referral codes, and subscription status.
  • Financial Records: Wallet balance, deposit requests, transaction IDs submitted by users, order charges, refunds, and internal transaction logs.
  • Order Data: Service identifiers, quantities, public profile or content links, order status, timestamps, and refill activity.
  • Device & Security Data: Hashed device fingerprints generated from IP address, browser user agent, and language headers for abuse prevention.
  • Technical Logs: Access logs, error logs, rate-limit events, and security-related activity.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To create and manage user accounts and sessions.
  • To process wallet deposits, subscriptions, and service orders.
  • To prevent fraud, abuse, duplicate free trials, and unauthorized access.
  • To enforce subscription limits and service eligibility rules.
  • To maintain platform stability, performance, and security.

3. Cookies & Session Management

We use secure, HTTP-only session cookies configured with appropriate security attributes. These cookies are strictly required for authentication and session integrity. We do not use tracking cookies or third-party advertising cookies.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Service Fulfillment: Only publicly provided links and order parameters are transmitted to external service providers to fulfill orders.
  • Exchange Rate APIs: Currency conversion data is fetched from external APIs without transmitting any personal user data.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share user personal data for marketing purposes.

5. Payments & Security

All wallet transactions operate on a manual verification model. We do not store credit card numbers, banking credentials, or payment passwords. Passwords are securely hashed using industry-standard cryptographic algorithms.

6. Data Retention

Account data is retained as long as the account remains active. Certain records may be retained after account closure where required for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or financial reconciliation.

7. User Rights

Users may request access to their stored data or request account deactivation through support. Abuse-related identifiers may be retained in hashed form to protect platform integrity.
